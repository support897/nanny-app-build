# App2 shell note — nav shape (founder ruling, 15 Oct 2026)

The app2 shell carries the NEW sidebar shape: hidden on load;
grouped areas; one area open at a time; when the menu opens, the
app content shrinks to a thin sliver at the edge and the menu
occupies most of the screen; tapping the sliver or closing
returns full content. The certified app keeps its sealed shape
until this replacement is proven and swapped.
